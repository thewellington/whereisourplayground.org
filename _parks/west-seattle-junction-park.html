Seattle Parks bought about 0.38 acres at 4723 40th SW more than a decade ago to add open space in the heart of the Junction. After COVID-era suspension and years of interim lawn use, the project went out to bid in June 2026. Parks now says construction is set to begin in early December 2026 (weather dependent), with the park planned to open in fall 2027. The West Seattle Blog is covering the schedule—and the city’s invitation to suggest a park name by November 20, 2026.

Welcome

Community members weighed in as early as 2017 on dozens of possible park features at an open-house style vote WSB covered at the Farmers Market, and saw concept boards again in 2018. In January 2019, a “you asked, we checked” piece quoted Parks putting the GGLO-led design at about 65 percent complete.

Parks suspended the capital job during the pandemic, then restored it through the 2023–2028 Park District cycle. An on-site design chat in October 2023 and later briefings still pointed years ahead—WSB’s November 2023 park-projects roundup referenced expectations of work around 2025. Interim use as open lawn continued while value engineering and permits caught up.

What’s building now: The finalized design keeps the community’s top asks—native plantings, a drinking fountain, and natural play—plus walking paths, lawn, seating, and a shaded concrete seat wall. WSB’s June 2026 bid story noted two elements (wooden boardwalk and seating stones) only if funding allows. Park District funding on the city page is about $3.014 million for planning, design, and construction; WSB has long noted the city paid about $1.4 million for the land in 2013.

Name it: Through Friday, November 20, 2026, Parks is taking written name suggestions that fit the park naming policy—email paula.hoff@seattle.gov or mail Seattle Parks and Recreation, Parks Naming Committee, 100 Dexter Ave. N, Seattle, WA 98109. Project contact: Fred.Gu@seattle.gov. Confirm schedule and design on the official project page.
