# Where Is Our Playground‽

Welcome to the project. Here we try to hold Seattle Parks accountable for the numerous delays and promises that they fail to keep. If you have a park or playground in mind that Seattle Parks has been ignoring for too long, or that Parks is hoping you are not paying attention to, please create a pull request against this repo with a new page detailing the situation in your park. Use the page to “get the information out” to your community, demanding that Seattle Parks take notice.

## Join us

- **Help maintain an existing page:** open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/thewellington/whereisourplayground.org/issues). Tell us who you are and which park you want to help with.

- **Suggest or add a new park:** open an [issue](https://github.com/thewellington/whereisourplayground.org/issues) describing the site or neighborhood, or send a pull request with a new park page when you are ready.

### Where content lives

- **New park pages** go in [`_parks/`](_parks/).
- Start from [`_parks/_template.html`](_parks/_template.html) and replace placeholders with real copy and links.
- **Images** for a park belong under `assets/images/<name-of-park>/` so they stay organized (and line up with Git LFS if your clone uses it).

### Running the site locally (developers)

If you want to preview the site or run a full build, see **[TECH_README.md](TECH_README.md)** for Nix, Ruby, Bundler, and Jekyll commands.

## Ideas to get Parks to pay attention

- Make stickers with the site URL `https://whereisourplayground.org` and a QR code that links to the site.
- Go to your neighborhood council or association and ask them to encourage Parks to pay attention to your park.
- Contact your council members and let them know you would like their help getting Parks to pay attention to your park.
