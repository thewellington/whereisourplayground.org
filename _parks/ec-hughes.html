E. C. Hughes Playground

2805 SW Holden · wading pool status changes with season and weather

{% include park-ribbon.html %}

A water-line failure at E. C. Hughes Playground (2805 SW Holden) kept the seasonal wading pool closed for much of summer 2024 while Seattle Parks and contractors replaced piping—including work with vertical boring to avoid a large open trench, per the West Seattle Blog. The pool reopened for a short late season on reduced hours before the usual mid-August wind-down. For current-year hours and status, use Parks’ wading pool list and morning hotline 206-684-7796.

Welcome

When wading-pool season started in June 2024, Hughes was still off the map—one fewer free splash spot for West Seattle families while crews chased leaks in the buried main. WSB quoted Parks describing a substantial line replacement, re-energizing the system, then another section failing—stretching the outage through the hottest weeks.

By early August 2024 the pool was back, but only Sundays, Mondays, and Tuesdays, noon–7 pm, with a brief run before the seasonal closing window described in WSB’s follow-up. Most West Seattle wading pools still wrap in mid-August; Lincoln Park’s pool is the one typically open daily through Labor Day.

This page isn’t just history: wading pools break, drain, and get weather-closed every year. When something looks wrong at Hughes, tip WSB and keep a dated record—neighbors notice outages before press releases do.

By The Numbers...

WSB’s June 2024 check-in treated June 22 as opening day for most city wading pools—while Hughes was still closed for repairs. Late-summer reopening bought only a slice of the season before standard August wind-downs.

For “how long is it closed this year,” track the same way WSB does: Parks’ public pool page, same-day visits, and the 206-684-7796 morning recording.
